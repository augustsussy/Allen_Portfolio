Case Study

GameNest

Rental Device Web App — Internship Capstone at IntelliSeven Technology Solutions Inc.

UI/UX Design Figma Web App KYC Flow

Role

UI/UX Designer

Type

Individual Capstone

Tool

Figma

Duration

Dec 2025 – Mar 2026

The Problem

Gamers couldn't access high-end devices affordably

High-end gaming and mobile devices are expensive — putting them out of reach for many gamers who only need them temporarily. There was no dedicated digital platform where users could browse available devices, verify their identity, and complete a rental in one streamlined flow.

GameNest was designed to solve this: a web app that lets gamers rent premium devices on demand, with a clear and trustworthy process from start to finish.

My Approach

Research, wireframes, then high-fidelity

I handled the entire design process end-to-end — from research to final prototype — as my individual internship capstone.

1

User research

Identified the target audience — gamers who want short-term access to premium devices — and mapped their key needs: easy device browsing, transparent pricing, and a trustworthy identity verification process.

2

Wireframing

Sketched low-fidelity wireframes to map out the full user journey before committing to visual decisions — covering login, device browsing, KYC verification, rental, and return flows.

3

High-fidelity design in Figma

Translated wireframes into a complete, interactive Figma prototype. Used a bold dark aesthetic suited to the gaming audience, while keeping each screen focused and uncluttered despite covering a large number of states and flows.

Key Design Challenge

Managing information density without cluttering screens

GameNest required a large number of screens covering many user states — renting for yourself vs. a minor, KYC with document uploads and face matching, device detail pages, payment flows, active rental timers, and inspection results. The challenge was fitting all of this information into each screen without overwhelming the user.

How I addressed it

I broke complex flows into focused, single-purpose screens — each screen asks the user to do only one thing. A consistent progress indicator across the KYC flow reduces anxiety around multi-step processes. Card-based layouts group related content and create visual breathing room, so screens feel organized even when they carry a lot of information.

Outcome

Approved, graded, and positively received

Delivered a full end-to-end Figma prototype covering all user flows as an individual internship capstone

Presented to and approved by supervisors at IntelliSeven Technology Solutions Inc.

Received positive feedback from the company on UX structure and visual design

Used as the primary graded output for my internship evaluation

About Me

Designer with a frontend edge based in the Philippines, open to full-time, part-time, and freelance work

I'm a UI/UX designer based in the Philippines, focused on creating interfaces that look polished and feel effortless to use. I work primarily in Figma from early wireframes all the way through to high-fidelity prototypes and I care about every detail that makes a design feel finished and professional.

I also have a frontend development background, which means I don't just design for how things look I think about how they actually get built. That combination lets me bridge the gap between design and code more effectively than most.

I recently completed my internship at IntelliSeven Technology Solutions Inc., where I delivered GameNest a full-featured gaming device rental platform as my individual capstone. It's the kind of project that pushed me to handle complex user flows, multiple screen states, and a cohesive dark UI aesthetic all at once.
